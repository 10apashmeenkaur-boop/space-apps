How accurate is it?

Published reference points (different setup, not a like-for-like comparison): the AI4Mars paper (CVPR 2021 workshop) reports DeepLabv3 at over 96% overall accuracy; the Zooniverse AI4Mars results report SPOCv2 at 91.41%. We do not claim to beat them. test_min3 scores only pixels that all labellers agreed on.

Limits: big rocks are very rare (0.1–0.2% of test pixels) and over-predicted (precision 0.11–0.26); labels reach ~30 m; 32×32-pixel cells blur thin edges; the image route is a camera-view preview.
